Detailed Description

The ScrumMaster/Stakeholder/EngineeringManager-weighted Business Summary for a single Initiative (Type C, parameterized for business audiences) - the other summary level actually printed in the report. Exactly one row per Initiative: overwritten fresh on every SummarizationService run.
